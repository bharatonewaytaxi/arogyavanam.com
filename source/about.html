— Our story

From Selling Land to Growing a Lifestyle

Saptha Ventures began in conventional real estate, delivering six RERA-approved plotted developments across Karnataka and the Karnataka–Andhra Pradesh border over more than a decade.

Recognising the growing desire for nature-inspired living, we expanded into managed farmland surrounded by lakes locks and natural flora and fauna.

Today, we manage over 300 acres, taking care of land surveying, documentation, fencing, plantation development, and ongoing maintenance — so you can own farmland without the everyday responsibilities of managing it.

— How we work

Three principles we never compromise on.

01 - Ownership

Land that's truly yours

Every parcel is carefully surveyed, legally verified, and registered in your name. Clear documentation, transparent ownership, and no uncertainty.

02 — Care

Farmland, managed

white sandalwood, casuarina tree fruit orchards and vegetable patches, our teams handle year-round planting, pruning, and harvesting to keep your land thriving while you're away.

03 — Place

A destination ready to enjoy

With roads, water, fencing, security, and a clubhouse planned as part of the estate, we focus on creating a well-maintained environment you can enjoy from your very first visit.
