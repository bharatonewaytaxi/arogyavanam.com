Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 October 2026

At Saptha Ventures, accessible through https://sapthaventures.com/, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our website, including:

  • Your full name.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number or contact number.
  • Information submitted through enquiry, callback, or contact forms.
  • Details about your interest in farmland projects, plantation services, or farm management.
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide when communicating with us.

We may also collect certain technical information automatically when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general website usage data, subject to applicable law and the technologies used on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Saptha Ventures may use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and callback requests.
  • To provide information about our managed farmland projects and related services.
  • To arrange site visits, consultations, and project discussions.
  • To communicate project updates, pricing information, and service details you have requested.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To maintain website security and prevent fraudulent or unauthorised activities.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information where permitted by applicable law, including when you provide consent, request information or services, or when processing is necessary to fulfil legal obligations or other legally recognised purposes.

Where consent is required, we will seek it in accordance with applicable law. You may withdraw your consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and any consequences explained at the time of withdrawal.

4. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share relevant information in limited circumstances, including:

  • With authorised employees and representatives who need the information to respond to your requests.
  • With service providers supporting website hosting, communication, analytics, or website maintenance.
  • With professional advisers or service partners where necessary to provide requested services.
  • With government authorities, regulators, or law enforcement agencies when required by applicable law.
  • Where you have provided consent or the disclosure is otherwise legally permitted.

Where appropriate, we take reasonable steps to ensure that third parties handle personal information responsibly and only for authorised purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand visitor behaviour, and enhance the browsing experience.

These technologies may help us:

  • Remember website preferences.
  • Understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Measure website performance and traffic.
  • Identify technical issues and improve website functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled. Where required, we will seek consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain relevant business records, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate record-retention requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about the personal data we process about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a concern or grievance regarding the handling of your personal information.
  • Exercise any other rights available to you under applicable data protection laws.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services. These external websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and practices.

Saptha Ventures is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals who can lawfully enquire about our projects and services. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without legally required consent, we will take appropriate steps to address the matter in accordance with applicable law.

11. Marketing Communications

We may contact you with project information, service updates, or promotional communications where permitted by applicable law and, where required, with your consent.

You may opt out of promotional communications by following the instructions provided in the communication or by contacting us directly. We may still send essential responses to enquiries or other service-related communications.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Saptha Ventures may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, website features, or applicable legal requirements.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy will be interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of India, including relevant data protection and privacy legislation, as applicable to our activities.
