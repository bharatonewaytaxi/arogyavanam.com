Our Story · Saptha Ventures

From Selling Land to Growing a Lifestyle

Saptha Ventures began in conventional real estate, delivering six RERA-approved plotted developments across Karnataka and the Karnataka–Andhra Pradesh border over more than a decade.

Recognising the growing desire for nature-inspired living, we expanded into managed farmland surrounded by lakes and natural flora and fauna. Today, we manage over 300 acres, taking care of land surveying, documentation, fencing, plantation development, and ongoing maintenance — so you can own farmland without the everyday responsibilities of managing it.

India’s first holistic managed farmland company, helping city families own, grow, and cherish organic farms.

FAQ

Everything you need to know about owning farmland

Can non-agriculturists purchase farmland in Andhra Pradesh?

Yes, non-agriculturists can purchase farmland in Andhra Pradesh.

Can I build a farmhouse on my farmland plot?

Farmhouse construction depends on land classification, local planning regulations and the required permissions, subject to the applicability and restrictions on the particular property.

How does farmland maintenance work when I live in the city?

You do not need to worry about day-to-day farming. Saptha Ventures manages the entire estate through our dedicated agricultural staff. We handle drip irrigation, weeding, fertilizer application, 24/7 gate security, and seasonal harvesting.

How are site visits arranged from Bengaluru?

Contact our team to schedule a convenient site visit. We can share the location, available visit timings, and the details you need before travelling.
