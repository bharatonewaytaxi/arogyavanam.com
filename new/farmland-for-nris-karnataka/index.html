Farmland for NRIs near Bengaluru

Many NRIs want a piece of green land near Bengaluru for family time and a place to reconnect with the land. Before you plan one, understand the rules.

What the rules say

Under India's foreign exchange rules (FEMA), NRIs and OCI cardholders generally cannot buy agricultural land, plantation property or farmhouses. They can usually hold such land only if they inherit it. Rules can change, so take advice from a qualified lawyer before you commit.

How NRI families usually take part

  • A resident Indian family member buys the plot in their own name
  • The NRI visits the farm and follows its progress remotely

Frequently asked questions

Can an NRI buy agricultural land in Karnataka?

Generally no. Under FEMA, NRIs and OCIs cannot buy agricultural land except by inheritance. Check with a lawyer for your case.

Can a family member in India buy instead?

Yes, a resident Indian can buy in their own name.
